A human-centric home for Python developers to do AI Our Way. Trusted, from us, for us.
Conversation, sharing, innovation, and building software tools.

## Why Trust Matters

Python developers are anxious. Their jobs, their craft, their world — AI is impacting all of it.
Some of us like it, but as a tool to make us better, not to replace us.

The trust deficit is real, and it comes from several directions:

- **Modern AI has become a megacorp game** to reduce developer headcount. We know coding
  agents can't (yet) replace developers. It's frustrating: not just the anti-engineering, but also
  the concentration of power in a handful of companies.
- **The "godbox" problem.** A lot of developers, especially the AI bros, just want a "godbox":
  type in some vague description of what you're looking for, get code that you won't look at,
  won't edit, won't understand, and won't truly own. We are not targeting that audience.
- **We want something open source.** Open weight models and open source agents are a start,
  but most "open" has investors behind it.
- **Like Python and Jupyter, we want something truly community-owned** — and there really
  isn't much. The people using it should decide where it goes. No single vendor owns the
  roadmap, and nothing important gets settled in a private channel.
- **Something European**, embedding the ethos and balance that folks worldwide associate
  with Europe. Built under European data and governance norms from day one.
- **Local-first for routine work**, using frontier and hosted models for big problems.
  I know my laptop. I trust my laptop.
- **The innovation that is possible** from the global Python community — the ones who
  actually built machine learning.
- **Most of all, a place for humans**, that respects smart talent and good hearts all over
  the globe.

## Local-First, Laptop AI

In a short period of time, smaller models have captured the imagination and are able to do
real work on powerful local computers. This trend will only continue.

However, getting onto a computer within reach of the global Python community remains
uncharted territory. Prices are sky-high and going up, meaning you have to rent a GPU — also
expensive — or, most likely, just stay with a non-trusted, non-local setup.

We want to target workflows where the big work is done on big brains' godbox, but *routine*
Python development can be done on the computer you have. Namely, a laptop. We can fill-in in
the big-brain gap using your actual developer brain. You then keep control, keep your hands
on the wheel.

Some folks have 128GB MacBook Pros, some have 64, 32 — but 16 is the dominant (before
getting to 8 GB, which might be difficult for now). These have tradeoffs: GPU, CPU, memory,
SSD speed, thermal, and of course, battery life.

[Local-First](local-first.md) covers what that means day to day.

We want to bring the power and innovation of the Python community to bear on this target:
AI Our Way, from us, for us.

## A Place for Humans

At the end of the day, the most important deliverable is community.

We want a place to hang out, to take back our power, to have fun with others and build
something we're proud of. A place to share progress and amplify innovation, as the Python
world continues to be a source of breakthroughs.

Thus, the first deliverable for SatyrnAI is a conversation: be a hub that organizes and
amplifies, encourages people to start looking at this, talking about this, pushing all this
forward.
