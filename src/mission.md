Satyrn is "Jupyter for agents": a project to build subagents and tooling that
helps Local AI succeed. From us, for us.

## The problem

Most AI tooling makes you pick a side. The polished stuff runs on someone
else's servers and you take its behaviour on faith. The open stuff you can read
end to end, and then you lose a weekend wiring it together.

We got tired of choosing.

## What we're building

!!! warning "Still being written"

    The roadmap below is a sketch, not a commitment. It will change once the
    shape of the first release is settled.

Agents that run on your own machine, and the unglamorous scaffolding around
them. The wiring. The defaults. The parts everyone currently rebuilds badly on
their own.

## What we care about

Everything is public. Fork it, audit it, ship it.

The people using it decide where it goes. No single vendor owns the roadmap,
and nothing important gets settled in a private channel.

It runs on your hardware. [Local-First](local-first.md) covers what that means
day to day.

It's built under European data and governance norms from day one.
