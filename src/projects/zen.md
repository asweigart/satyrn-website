Satyrn Zen is about building a community of teachers and learners. We want to give people opportunities to grow their careers, share with others, and build AI Our Way.

Topics we care about include model training, evaluation harnesses, engine development, and more — anything that helps Python developers understand and shape the tools they use every day.

## Coming Soon

This is an early vision, and we're still figuring out the best ways to make it real. Here are some of the things we might do:

- **Workshops and tutorials** — hands-on sessions on model training, evaluation, and engine development.
- **Mentorship programs** — pairing experienced practitioners with those starting out.
- **Study groups** — collaborative learning around papers, tools, and techniques.
- **Talks and meetups** — sharing knowledge and building connections in the Python AI community.
- **Learning resources** — curated guides, articles, and videos on topics from basics to advanced.

We'll share more as these take shape. In the meantime, if you're interested in teaching, learning, or helping us figure out what the community needs, [get involved](../get-involved.md).
